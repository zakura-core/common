## zakura-halo2-proofs

### Changed

- Reduced four-Action Orchard prover latency by committing constant-prefix
  permutation products from their constant coefficient, blinded-tail deltas,
  and commitment blind. At k=11, each hit replaces a 2,049-term Lagrange
  commitment with a prefix scan and at most seven terms. This reduced
  four-Action proof time by 0.202 ms on Apple M4 with 10 workers and 0.556 ms
  on AMD Linux with 6 workers; one-Action latency remained neutral
  ([#359](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/359)).
