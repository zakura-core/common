## zakura-halo2-proofs

### Changed

- Reduced deferred-IPA generator materialization work by widening its prepared
  wNAF table from seven to eight bits. For Orchard this removes about 6,755
  mixed curve additions and 422 KiB of affine-point reads per proof. The table
  grows from 3.75 MiB to 7.5 MiB; with 10 workers on Apple M4, materialization
  improved by 0.148 ms and complete proofs by 0.274 ms. With 6 workers,
  materialization improved by 0.193 ms on Apple M4 and 0.328 ms on x86-64
  Linux
  ([#425](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/425)).
