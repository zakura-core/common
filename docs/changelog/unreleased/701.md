## zakura-pasta-curves

### Changed

- On x86-64 builds with AVX2 (an AVX2 target, or the forced `x86_64-asm`
  backend), large GLV multiscalar multiplications reduce their buckets with
  four-way AVX2 affine additions, about 30% faster per addition
  ([#701](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/701)).
