Keep the fixed-base preparation for sparse MSM inputs while recoding and
scanning only their nonzero rows. Eight real four-Action lookup-difference
MSMs improved by 102.05 us on an Apple M4 with ten workers and 155.06 us on
six-worker x86-64 Linux; the semantic-cold four-Action proof improved by
1.40 ms on Linux and was directionally faster on M4. One-Action proofs were
neutral. The change adds no retained cache and preserves proof bytes and
public APIs.
