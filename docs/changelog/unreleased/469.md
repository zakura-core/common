Use blocked deferred inner products for parallel AArch64 multi-opening
collapse. On an Apple M4 with ten workers, the exact one-Action kernel fell
from 337.41 us to 279.20 us (17.25%), and the four-Action kernel fell from
574.82 us to 443.78 us (22.80%). Proof bytes, retained memory, non-AArch64 and
single-worker behavior, and public APIs are unchanged.
