## zakura-orchard

### Added

- Added `Action::batch_from_encodings`, with `ActionEncoding` and
  `ActionDecodeError`, which builds Actions from their wire encodings,
  decoding all of their points together and accepting exactly what
  `Action::from_parts` accepts after one-at-a-time decoding
  ([#731](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/731)).

## zakura-reddsa

### Added

- Added `From<pallas::Affine>` for Orchard spend authorization verification
  keys ([#731](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/731)).

## zakura-primitives

### Changed

- Sped up reading Orchard bundles by decoding all of a bundle's Action points
  together; when a bundle has several errors, the one reported may differ
  ([#731](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/731)).
