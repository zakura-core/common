Single-Action proofs now reuse explicitly related rational-advice
denominators, reducing the measured cold-proof rational evaluation by 0.055 ms
on Apple M4 with ten workers and 0.179 ms on x86-64 Linux with six workers and
assembly. Ironwood retains 3,257 instead of 7,007 denominators, removing
270,000 bytes of logical transient denominator, cell-index, and inversion
scratch payload before small relation metadata. This changes no proof, circuit,
format, or public or `pub(crate)` API.
