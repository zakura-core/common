## zakura-halo2-proofs

### Changed

- Reduced sparse masking commitment time during proof generation by 3–30% in
  isolated one- and six-worker benchmarks while using a three-coefficient
  quadratic quotient mask. The mask retains one random coefficient of excess
  entropy after its two protocol evaluations are fixed; complete six-worker
  Orchard proofs improved by 0.1–0.2% on Apple M4 and other complete-proof
  results were below measurement noise
  ([#294](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/294)).
