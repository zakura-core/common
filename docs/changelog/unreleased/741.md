## zakura-pasta-curves

### Changed

- On x86-64 builds with AVX2 (an AVX2 target, or the forced `x86_64-asm`
  backend), large GLV multiscalar multiplications reduce their buckets with
  eight-way AVX2 affine additions, and each thread keeps up to 64 MiB of
  window scratch between MSMs
  ([#741](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/741)).
