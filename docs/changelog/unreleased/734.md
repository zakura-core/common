## zakura-halo2-proofs

### Changed

- Sped up `BatchVerifier::finalize` for batches that mix one or a few large
  proofs with many small ones, by decoding a dominant proof's points across
  the worker pool instead of on one worker; an Orchard batch of one
  75-Action bundle and 15 2-Action bundles verifies about 30% faster on 8
  threads ([#734](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/734)).
