## zakura-halo2-proofs

### Changed

- Reduced prepared `k = 11` multi-circuit proving latency on pools with at
  least eight workers in `multicore` builds without `orbits` by starting advice
  polynomial transforms while the sparse advice-delta planner selects and
  evaluates commitments. Proof format, RNG consumption, and transcript
  ordering are unchanged
  ([#353](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/353)).
