## zakura-pasta-curves

### Changed

- With the `multicore` feature, large GLV multiscalar multiplications
  (4,096 terms or more) prepare their scalars and bases on the Rayon thread
  pool ([#692](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/692)).

## zakura-halo2-proofs

### Changed

- With the `multicore` feature, batch verification prepares large batches'
  accumulated commitment terms for the final MSM on the Rayon thread pool,
  making large batches faster on multi-core machines
  ([#692](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/692)).
