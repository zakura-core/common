## zakura-primitives

### Changed

- `TransactionData::check_v7_transaction_rules` now also rejects a coinbase
  transaction with Orchard actions, an Orchard or Ironwood bundle with
  neither spends nor outputs enabled, and a transparent input with a null
  previous output, with the new `v7::TransactionRuleError` variants
  `CoinbaseOrchardActions`, `NoSpendsOrOutputs`, and `NullPrevout`. Its
  documentation now says that the coinbase delta has no term for the value
  ZIP 271 pays out of the lockbox, which ZIP 248 leaves out, and that the
  check leaves to full validators the limit of 65,535 Sapling spends,
  Sapling outputs, and Orchard or Ironwood actions, a previous output or
  nullifier that the transaction repeats, and the total value of the
  transparent outputs of a transaction with transparent inputs
  ([#758](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/758)).
