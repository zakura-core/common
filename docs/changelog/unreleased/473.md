Initialize each batched sparse-permutation product from its first factor instead
of multiplying that factor by one. Ironwood has 889 active rows across its two
sparse sets, so this removes 1,778 field multiplications per Action and 7,112
for four Actions. Focused four-Action preparation improved by 22.45 us on an
Apple M4 with ten workers and 25.35 us on x86-64 Linux with six workers and
`x86_64-asm`. One-Action proving does not use this batched path and is unchanged.
Proof bytes, retained memory, and public APIs are unchanged.
