## zakura-pasta-curves

### Added

- `arithmetic::CoefficientProducts`, implemented for `Fp` and `Fq`: sums of
  inner-product-argument challenge-product coefficient vectors, expanded and
  accumulated with AVX2 on x86-64 builds that enable it
  ([#714](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/714)).

## zakura-halo2-proofs

### Changed

- Batch verification sums the proofs' IPA generator coefficients without a
  per-proof coefficient vector, roughly halving that step's cost on AVX2
  builds ([#714](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/714)).
