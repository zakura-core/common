## zakura-protocol

### Added

- `SLH_DSA_KEY_COMMITMENT_DOMAIN`, the domain separator for experimental
  SLH-DSA public-key commitments
  ([#646](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/646)).

## zakura-transparent

### Changed

- Experimental post-quantum accounts now sign with SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s
  (FIPS 205) through the new `pq::scheme::SlhDsa` scheme instead of WOTS+,
  which remains available as `pq::scheme::WotsPlus`. Account keys, key-set
  roots and authorization sizes change. Added the `pq_schemes` benchmark
  comparing the schemes
  ([#646](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/646)).
