Use inline point masks while grouping prover multi-opening queries. Orchard has
69 distinct commitments for one Action and 138 for four Actions, so this
removes 207 and 414 tiny index-vector allocations respectively. In focused
paired measurements, the serial grouping step improved by 12.54 us for one
Action and 31.11 us for four Actions on an Apple M4 with ten workers, and by
24.18 us and 65.19 us on x86-64 Linux with six workers and `x86_64-asm`.
Proof bytes, field and group arithmetic, retained memory, verifier behavior,
and public APIs are unchanged.
