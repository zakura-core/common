## zakura-orchard

### Changed

- **Breaking:** `builder::Builder::{build, build_for_pczt}`, `builder::bundle`,
  and `builder::OutputInfo::dummy` now require `CryptoRng` in addition to `Rng`.
  Callers must use a securely seeded cryptographic RNG
  ([#632](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/632)).

## zakura-sapling-crypto

### Changed

- **Breaking:** `builder::Builder::{build, build_coinbase, build_for_pczt}`,
  `builder::bundle`, `builder::OutputInfo::dummy`, and
  `note_encryption::sapling_note_encryption` now require `CryptoRng` in addition
  to `Rng`. Callers must use a securely seeded cryptographic RNG
  ([#632](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/632)).

## zakura-note-encryption

### Changed

- **Breaking:** `NoteEncryption::encrypt_outgoing_plaintext` now requires
  `CryptoRng` in addition to `Rng`, including when an outgoing viewing key is
  supplied. Callers must use a securely seeded cryptographic RNG
  ([#632](https://github.com/zakura-core/common/pull/632)).
