Reduce the scratch used to recode prepared-MSM residual tails by deriving their
digit-row stride from the existing residual bound. For a 2,048-term alpha-7
MSM, this avoids zeroing 163,840 bytes and reduces the isolated tail kernel by
10.77 us on Apple M4 and 20.80 us on six-worker x86-64 Linux. The serial M4
full MSM improved by 27.83 us, while multicore full-MSM measurements were
neutral. Retained memory, proof results, and public APIs are unchanged.
